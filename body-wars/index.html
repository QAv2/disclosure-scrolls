The Flexner Report (1910)

Documented
In 1910, the Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching published a report by Abraham Flexner — an educator with no medical credentials whose brother, Simon Flexner, directed the Rockefeller Institute — surveying all 155 medical schools across the United States and Canada (148 in the U.S.). The report recommended closing most of them. The American Medical Association (AMA) Council on Medical Education had privately requested the Carnegie survey in 1907, documented in its own minutes — seeking an outside body, as historian Paul Starr documented, to restrict physician supply and eliminate competing schools.
Flexner, Abraham. Medical Education in the United States and Canada, Carnegie Foundation Bulletin No. 4 (1910, PDF) (Internet Archive full text). AMA Council on Medical Education minutes, 1907. Starr, Paul. The Social Transformation of American Medicine, 1982 (Pulitzer Prize).

While the Carnegie Foundation funded the 1910 report itself, Rockefeller’s General Education Board (GEB) hired Abraham Flexner in 1913 and distributed over $91 million (~$2 billion in 2026 dollars across cumulative 1919–1960 grants) to medical schools that adopted the laboratory-clinical biomedical model. Documented

The Fluoride Question

Documented
Roughly 90% of U.S. water fluoridation uses fluorosilicic acid — a byproduct captured from the smokestack scrubbers of phosphate fertilizer plants (regulated for purity under NSF/ANSI Standard 60). The CDC’s own materials acknowledge it is “a byproduct of the manufacture of phosphate fertilizers.” The U.S. Public Health Service issued a preliminary endorsement of fluoridation in 1950 (formalized in 1951) — only 5 years into a planned 15-year safety study in Grand Rapids, Michigan.
CDC Water Fluoridation Additives; NSF/ANSI Standard 60; NIDCR: The Story of Fluoridation (Grand Rapids study).
Documented
Analyzing 72 human studies, the final August 2024 National Toxicology Program (NTP) monograph concluded with “moderate confidence” that higher fluoride exposures — specifically drinking-water levels above 1.5 mg/L, more than double the 0.7 mg/L U.S. community fluoridation standard — are associated with lower IQ in children (while noting insufficient data to determine whether 0.7 mg/L alone lowers IQ). Applying the Toxic Substances Control Act’s (TSCA) statutory margin-of-safety framework, U.S. District Judge Edward Chen ruled on September 24, 2024 in Food & Water Watch, Inc. v. EPA that fluoridation at 0.7 mg/L poses “an unreasonable risk of injury to health” — the first federal court ruling against community water fluoridation in American history.
Food & Water Watch, Inc. v. EPA, Case No. 3:17-cv-02162-EMC (N.D. Cal., Sept. 24, 2024); NTP Monograph on Fluoride Exposure (August 2024) (DOI: 10.22427/NTP-MGRAPH-8).
Update · November 2026

On May 21, 2026, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit (Food & Water Watch, Inc. v. EPA, No. 25-384) vacated Judge Edward Chen’s September 2024 judgment on procedural grounds — holding that the district court abused its discretion under the party-presentation principle by holding the 2020 trial open for years to incorporate the August 2024 NTP monograph — and remanded the case to be decided on the original trial record. The appellate panel did not rule on fluoride’s safety. Meanwhile, state legislatures moved independently: Utah and Florida enacted the first statewide bans on adding fluoride to public drinking water in 2025, while HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. directed the CDC to revise federal fluoridation guidance and the EPA initiated a formal toxicity review.

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""
Extract files hidden in a CNTYSEED steganographic image.
Only requires: pip install Pillow
Usage: python3 extract.py <image.png> <output_dir>
"""
import hashlib, io, os, struct, sys, tarfile, zlib
from PIL import Image

MAGIC = b"CNTYSEED"
HDR_SIZE = 64
HDR_FMT = "!8sBBBBII32sHH8s"


def lsb_decode(flat, depth, num_bytes):
    total_bits = num_bytes * 8
    pad = depth - (total_bits % depth) if total_bits % depth else 0
    slots = (total_bits + pad) // depth
    mask = (1 << depth) - 1
    bits = []
    for i in range(slots):
        val = flat[i] & mask
        for shift in range(depth - 1, -1, -1):
            bits.append((val >> shift) & 1)
    out = bytearray()
    for i in range(0, num_bytes * 8, 8):
        byte = 0
        for b in range(8):
            byte = (byte << 1) | bits[i + b]
        out.append(byte)
    return bytes(out)


def extract(image_path, output_dir):
    img = Image.open(image_path).convert("RGB")
    flat = [ch for px in img.getdata() for ch in px]

    hdr = None
    for depth in (1, 2, 3, 4):
        raw = lsb_decode(flat, depth, HDR_SIZE)
        if raw[:8] != MAGIC:
            continue
        fields = struct.unpack(HDR_FMT, raw)
        if fields[3] == depth:
            hdr = {
                "flags": fields[2], "lsb_depth": depth,
                "compression": fields[4], "original_size": fields[5],
                "compressed_size": fields[6], "sha256": fields[7],
            }
            break

    if not hdr:
        print("No embedded data found.")
        sys.exit(1)

    if hdr["flags"] & 0x01:
        print("This image is encrypted.")
        sys.exit(1)

    print(f"Found: {hdr['original_size']:,} bytes, {hdr['lsb_depth']}-LSB")

    total = HDR_SIZE + hdr["compressed_size"]
    raw = lsb_decode(flat, hdr["lsb_depth"], total)
    payload = raw[HDR_SIZE:]

    if hdr["compression"] == 1:
        payload = zlib.decompress(payload)
    elif hdr["compression"] == 2:
        import lzma
        payload = lzma.decompress(payload)

    if hashlib.sha256(payload).digest() == hdr["sha256"]:
        print("Integrity: VERIFIED")
    else:
        print("WARNING: SHA-256 mismatch")

    os.makedirs(output_dir, exist_ok=True)
    if hdr["flags"] & 0x02:
        with tarfile.open(fileobj=io.BytesIO(payload), mode="r") as tar:
            tar.extractall(path=output_dir, filter="data")
        print(f"Extracted to: {output_dir}/")
    else:
        with open(os.path.join(output_dir, "extracted"), "wb") as f:
            f.write(payload)
        print(f"Written to: {output_dir}/extracted")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    if len(sys.argv) != 3:
        print(f"Usage: {sys.argv[0]} <image.png> <output_dir>")
        sys.exit(1)
    extract(sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2])
